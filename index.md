I'm passionate about leveraging data science to drive sustainable solutions within the energy and built environment sectors.

I completed my PhD in the <a href="https://bseg.cae.drexel.edu/" target="_blank"><u>Building Science and Engineering Group (BSEG)</u></a> led by <a href="https://drexel.edu/engineering/about/faculty-staff/W/wen-jin/" target="_blank"><u>Professor Jin Wen</u></a> at <a href="https://drexel.edu/" target="_blank"><u>Drexel University</u></a>. My doctoral research focused on developing data-driven strategies to optimize performance and control of smart <a href="https://www.energy.gov/eere/buildings/grid-interactive-efficient-buildings" target="_blank"><u>Grid-interactive Efficient Buildings (GEBs)</u></a>.

In my current role, I specialize in developing and deploying end-to-end machine learning algorithms to orchestrate Distributed Energy Resources, supporting the energy transition and net-zero goals.
