I completed my PhD in the <a href="https://bseg.cae.drexel.edu/" target="_blank">Building Science and Engineering Group (BSEG)</a> led by <a href="https://drexel.edu/engineering/about/faculty-staff/W/wen-jin/" target="_blank">Professor Jin Wen</a> at <a href="https://drexel.edu/" target="_blank">Drexel University</a>. My doctoral research focused on developing data-driven strategies to optimize performance and control of smart <a href="https://www.energy.gov/eere/buildings/grid-interactive-efficient-buildings" target="_blank">Grid-interactive Efficient Buildings (GEBs)</a>.

I'm passionate about leveraging data science to drive sustainable solutions in the built environment. In my current role, I specialise on developing and deploying end-to-end data analytics, machine learning algorithms, and optimised engineering research strategies to reduce energy usage and carbon emissions in commercial buildings. I am continuously expanding my expertise in data science and sustainable building methodologies to contribute to a greener future.
