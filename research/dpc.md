<div class="row-md-6" style="text-align: justify">    
    In recent years, building controls have become increasingly important with the rapid development of grid-interactive efficient buildings. Model predictive control (MPC) which is an optimal control-based method that selects control inputs by minimizing an objective function has been widely studied. For the MPC to perform accurately, a precise building energy forecasting model is essential. Data-driven models which rely purely on the data from sensors in the building automation system (BAS) to capture the relationship between building operation data and energy consumption have shown great potential in recent years. Data-driven approaches can efficiently characterize system operations and develop accurate system models that are automated, scalable, and require low engineering time and labor cost.
