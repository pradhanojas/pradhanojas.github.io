<div class="row-md-6" style="text-align: justify">
    Buildings are subject to faults in their heating, ventilation and air-conditioning (HVAC) systems that can lead to excessive energy wastage, poor indoor climate, equipment failures and high maintenance costs.  Field studies have shown that employing fault detection and diagnosis (FDD) tools followed up with equipment services and corrections can help achieve 10-40% of energy savings within the HVAC system and improve indoor climate, increase equipment lifecycle and reduce maintenance costs. Within the FDD framework, fault detection determines whether the HVAC system has failures or incorrect operations in a system, whereas, fault diagnosis process localizes the root cause of the faulty operation. <br />  
    
    <a href="https://www.energy.gov/eere/buildings/articles/securing-grid-interactive-efficient-buildings-geb-through-cyber-defense-and" target="blank"> CYDRES Project - Funded by US Department of Energy </a> <br />  
    
    Research, development, and demonstration a real-time advanced building resilient platform, called CYber Defense and REsilient System (CYDRES), deployable for existing and emerging Building Automation Systems (BAS), to empower grid-interactive buildings with cyber-attack-immune capabilities through multi-layer prevention, detection, and adaptation.
